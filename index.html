DELTA SINGULAR

Special Building — Kryoneri, Attica, Greece

Architect: Xenophon Stefanidis

Project Overview

The DELTA PLIROFORIKI S.A. project in Kryoneri, Attica, involved the remodelling and extension of an existing industrial building into a high-tech, maximum-security facility.

The project documentation describes specialised functions including intelligent printing, a bank disaster room, credit-card embossing, a call center for disabled employees, restaurant and kitchen facilities, kindergarten, infirmary, physical therapy and gym areas.

Architectural Scope

According to the confirmation issued by AKTOR S.A. on 25 July 2007, Xenophon Stefanidis provided the architectural pre-design and detail design, site supervision, interior pre-design and detail design, and supervision of the project.

Project Scale

The confirmation states that the relevant project value was approximately €13,000,000, with an architectural design component of approximately €7,000,000. The overall covered area was approximately 10,000 m² and the project was launched during 2000–2001.
